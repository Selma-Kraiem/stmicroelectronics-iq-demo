# AEC-Q101 Requirements — Summary (extract)

Document type: Automotive qualification requirements summary
Owner: Quality & Reliability Engineering — Fictional demo data

Summary of the AEC-Q101 stress-qualification framework applicable to automotive discrete
power semiconductors (SiC MOSFET), used as the normative frame for containment and customer
notification decisions.

## Key points relevant to an excursion
- Parametric parameters (Vth, Rds(on), Igss) must remain within qualified limits across the
  full temperature and lifetime stress profile.
- A parameter excursion beyond spec on an automotive lot requires:
  1. Immediate containment of affected and adjacent lots.
  2. Root-cause analysis (8D).
  3. Customer notification if parts have shipped and a field-reliability risk cannot be excluded.
- Zero-defect mindset (PPAP / IATF 16949 context): safety-critical applications such as EV
  traction demand a conservative disposition.

## Disposition principle
Automotive-grade parts under AEC-Q101 with a safety-critical end use (e.g. EV traction
inverter) require conservative disposition and prompt customer notification whenever field
exposure exists.
