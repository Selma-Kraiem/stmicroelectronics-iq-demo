# V2 presenter script

1. Open the dedicated
   [Chat](https://st-iq-v2.example.com/v2/chat)
   page and point out that Commander is the single entry point.
2. Ask: “What current public signals could affect replacement supply?”
   Highlight the Market route and the `Web Knowledge Source (Web IQ fallback)` badge.
3. Ask: “Which lots and fictional customers are exposed?” Highlight the Quality route,
   Foundry IQ citations, and the two structured operational APIs.
4. Open the separate
   [Tasks](https://st-iq-v2.example.com/v2/tasks)
   page and start the full SiC incident assessment.
5. In **Agents and tools used**, follow the common incident/run IDs through Commander
   fan-out, both A2A branches, Foundry IQ, Web Knowledge Source, Tool Search calls to the
   Manufacturing Telemetry and Customer Exposure APIs, and cited fan-in. Point out that
   live calls are labelled `TOOL`; deterministic fallbacks are labelled `ADAPTER`.
6. In the final brief, distinguish facts, hypotheses, missing evidence, fictional customer
   impact, and recommended containment actions.
7. Open the Teams preview. Emphasize that it is a dry run and that Work IQ Teams is visibly
   disabled by the tenant license check; no message is sent.

The scenario is fictional. Lot `CAT-26-0813-A`, all operational records, customers, orders,
inventory, telemetry, and internal procedures are deterministic synthetic demo data.
